# Store listing

## Title

VolumeBuster: Volume Booster

## Short description

Make every app louder than 100%: volume boost, bass and equalizer. No root.

## Full description

VolumeBuster raises the sound of all apps above the system maximum: music, video, podcasts, games and browsers.
No root and no special setup: turn the boost on and play whatever you like.

VOLUME BOOST
• From 100% to 150% (up to +10.6 dB) in 5% steps.
• The gain changes gradually, without clicks or sudden jumps.
• Quick Settings tile: switch the boost on and off without opening the app.

BASS AND EQUALIZER
• Bass from −9 to +9 dB.
• Equalizer with 5, 7 or 10 bands.
• Presets: Flat, Acoustic, Classical, Jazz, Pop, Rock, Hip-hop, Dance, Voice, plus your own curve.

RELIABLE IN THE BACKGROUND
• Keeps working while other apps play.
• Remembers your settings and restores them when you open the app again, for example after a reboot.
• Switching off brings back the normal sound at once.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No Internet permission: nothing leaves your phone.
• No ads, no analytics, no accounts.
• Open source under the MIT license.

Diagnostics show where the sound goes, the current media volume and the active effect, so you can see what your
device supports.

Please note
• Loud sound can damage your hearing and the speaker. Start with a small boost.
• Requires Android 12 or newer. Some manufacturers do not allow effects on the system-wide sound; the app tells you
  if this is the case.
• Another equalizer or booster app that controls the system sound can block VolumeBuster. Turn its effects off.
