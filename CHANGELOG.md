## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-12

First public release.

### Added

- Volume boost above the system maximum for every app, without root: 100–150%.
- Bass control from −9 to +9 dB.
- Equalizer with 5, 7 or 10 bands and a custom curve.
- Quick Settings tile to turn the boost on and off without opening the app.
- Keeps working in the background while other apps play.
- The boost, equalizer and bass are restored when the app is opened again, e.g. after a reboot.
- Diagnostics: current audio output, media volume, active effect and gain, system effects.
- Hearing and speaker warning when the boost is first switched on.
- English, Russian and German interface; on Android 13+ the language can be chosen per app.
- Light and dark theme following the system.
